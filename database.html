Privacy Policy

Draft for prototype
This is a prototype privacy policy. Before launch, it should be reviewed by a qualified GDPR/privacy professional.

What we collect

We may collect account information such as name, surname, email address, phone number, company, role/title, password credentials, communication preferences, saved favourites, and submitted updates.

Player profile information

The database may contain football-related public profile information such as player name, date of birth/age, nationality, position, current club, league, transfer history, contract status where lawfully available, and public source links.

Sources and verification

Users may submit source links such as club websites, federation pages, league pages, match reports, official announcements, or other publicly available football sources. Proposed updates are reviewed before becoming verified platform data.

Under-18 players

Players under the age of 18 are not displayed publicly. A player aged 17 may be submitted for review, but the profile remains archived and hidden from public display until their 18th birthday. Under-17 player submissions are not accepted in this prototype policy.

Legal basis

For account users, processing is based on providing the platform service and user consent for optional communications. For public professional football data, processing may rely on legitimate interest, balanced against the rights and freedoms of the individual.

Your rights

Individuals may request access, correction, restriction, objection, or deletion of their personal data. Players may request corrections to inaccurate profile information or ask for a review of public display.

If you wish to have your data removed, please contact emelia@the12thplayer.net and we will action your removal request.

Communication preferences

Platform updates and relevant player news are optional. Users must opt in and can unsubscribe or update preferences at any time.

Data separation

Public database data is separate from private 12th Player CRM data. Private scouting notes, internal lists, and organisation-specific workflows are not publicly displayed.

Contact

For privacy requests, corrections, or data removal requests, use the Contact page or email: emelia@the12thplayer.com.
